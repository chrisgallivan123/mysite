# 🚀 Viz Turbo

Flow metrics are necessary — but not sufficient. They reveal how work moves, not whether your organization is learning faster. With AI adoption now top of mind, Viz Turbo brings these two forces together. It’s an AI‑first flow metrics dashboard that pairs core delivery signals (velocity, flow time, efficiency, load) with real‑time AI usage and impact, so leaders can see where AI is taking hold, how it’s changing outcomes, and where to invest next.

## Features

- **Real-Time Trend Indicators**: Track velocity, flow time, efficiency, load, and AI adoption trends with color-coded indicators
- **Key Metrics**: Flow velocity, flow time, flow efficiency, flow load, and AI adoption tracking
- **Advanced Visualizations**: Stacked area charts, line charts, pie charts, and AI vs Non-AI performance comparisons
- **CSV Data Import**: Load flow metrics data from CSV files with flexible formatting
