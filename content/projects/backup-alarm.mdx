# Backup Alarm: Never Miss a Wake-Up Again

When you set an alarm before bed, you're making a trust contract with your phone: wake me up at this time. But phones die. Batteries drain overnight. Software crashes silently. And the failure mode is uniquely cruel — you don't know the alarm failed until you wake up late.

This project started from personal pain. Almost missed an EMEA call after my phone died overnight. My kids have dance competitions with 5 AM call times — if those alarms don't fire, they don't compete. There's no "join late" in dance.

## The Solution: Two Features, One Safety Net

### Battery Risk Prediction & Nudge

When you toggle on an alarm, the system predicts whether your battery will survive until alarm time. If it won't, you see a nudge immediately — while you can still plug in. The AI isn't a bolt-on. The AI IS the feature.

### Cross-Device Backup Cascade

Configure a backup list — Watch, HomePod, family member's phone. If your phone doesn't respond at alarm time, backups fire in sequence until someone wakes up.

```
Your alarm: 5:00 AM

If phone fails:
  5:02 — Apple Watch fires
  5:04 — HomePod fires (if no response)
  5:06 — Jen's iPhone fires (if still no response)
```

## Why This Project Matters

This was designed as a product management exercise, but I used it to demonstrate a methodology:

- **AI-first, not AI-second** — the AI is the product, not a feature added later
- **Validate riskiest assumptions first** — can it predict battery death? Can it send a backup ping? Does it deliver peace of mind?
- **Show the work** — every decision documented with alternatives considered and reasoning
- **Prototype early** — working mockups before the spec was "done"

Built end-to-end with Claude Code in 48 hours: spec, architecture decision records, user stories, working prototypes, and persona validation with my family.
