---
title: "Bank Platform: From Baseline to Early Signals"
excerpt: "Baseline, enable, show early flow-time improvements; scale to 8–10 product groups."
---

**Challenge.** Large product groups with diffuse priorities.

**Moves.**
- Establish baseline and *visible* goals (flow time -10%).
- Enable in Viz; add context capture to see *why*.
- Weekly huddles on SDI outliers + “gold” tags.

**Impact.**
- Early downward shift in average flow time.
- Fewer hidden pileups after aligning intake limits.
